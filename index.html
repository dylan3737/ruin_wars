Quests

Spend Energy on jobs. Earn gold and XP. Mastery ranks unlock at 5 / 15 / 40 completions. Avalon opens at Level 10, the Wastes at Level 20.

The Lists

Spend Stamina to face rivals on the Field of Honor. Wounds cost Health — Rest at the Chapel when fallen.

Chapel Rest

Spend gold to mend wounds. Fights are locked while Health is 0.

Bosses

Great foes with vast HP. Each strike costs Stamina; the wounds you deal persist between strikes and heal only slowly. Slay them for great rewards, then wait for their return.

Knight Band

Knights who ride under your banner. Each raises your attack and defense in The Lists and against bosses. How many sworn knights you can lead grows with your level.

Holdings

Buy lands and towers. They gather gold over real time — Collect before the coffers overflow (2 cycles).

Inventory

Loot from quests, fights and bosses. Equip one weapon and one armor; relics grant passive bonuses while kept.

Collections

Set pieces drop from quests in each region, from fight wins, and always from bosses. Duplicates are kept. Complete a set to redeem it once for a permanent bonus (uses one of each piece).

Armory & Relics

Buy once. Bonuses stack into every quest thereafter.
